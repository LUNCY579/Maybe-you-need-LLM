LLM Materials

The collection, as textbooks

Every course, compiled end-to-end into one readable volume — the lesson prose, the mathematics with derivations, the practice questions with their answer key, the laboratory work, and the glossary. The math renders with KaTeX; the courses themselves remain the primary artefact, because the laboratories run in the browser.

A suggested order

The levels are positions on the learning path, not difficulty ratings: a course at level 2 assumes one thing the level-1 courses teach. Any course can be read on its own.

  1. Level 1 — LLM Foundations Lab · Deep Learning Theory Explorer · Reinforcement Learning Foundations
  2. Level 2 — Language Model from Scratch · Diffusion Lab · Evaluation Lab · Retrieval Lab · Multimodal Lab
  3. Level 3 — Scaling Lab · Mechanistic Interpretability Lab · RLHF Lab · Systems Lab · Agent Benchmark Lab
  4. Level 4 — Data Lab · Agentic AI Academy · Safety and Alignment Lab
